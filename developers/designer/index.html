Designer Guide

Neurodesk Design Style Guide

Section titled “Neurodesk Design Style Guide”

This style guide provides clear standards to ensure visual consistency across all Neurodesk platforms and materials.

Brand overview

Section titled “Brand overview”

Neurodesk is a community-oriented open-source solution for neuroimaging analysis with four guiding principles: accessibility, portability, flexibility and, overarchingly, reproducibility. This guide ensures visual consistency across all Neurodesk products and documentation.

Where these files live

Section titled “Where these files live”

All Neurodesk visual assets are stored in the neurodesk/media repository, which is the single source of truth. This page explains how to use them; the repository holds the files themselves.

If an asset is missing, or you need a variant that doesn’t exist, open an issue rather than editing an existing file.

Licensing and permitted use

Section titled “Licensing and permitted use”

Two different sets of terms apply, and the distinction matters.

WhatTerms
Figures, diagrams, and screenshotsCC BY 4.0 — reuse freely with attribution
The Neurodesk name and logoProject marks — not CC BY

Attribution string: Neurodesk (https://neurodesk.org), CC BY 4.0

You may, without asking

Section titled “You may, without asking”
  • Use an unmodified logo to link to neurodesk.org or a Neurodesk repository.
  • Use an unmodified logo in slides, posters, papers, or blog posts that describe, teach, review, or cite Neurodesk.
  • State truthfully that your tool, pipeline, or course uses or supports Neurodesk.
  • Scale the logo proportionally, and choose whichever variant reads best on your background.

Please ask first

Section titled “Please ask first”

Open an issue on neurodesk/media, or email mail.neurodesk@gmail.com, before you:

  • Change the logo’s colours, proportions, typography, or artwork.
  • Combine it with another mark into a new lockup.
  • Use it on merchandise, event branding, or in a domain name.
  • Use it in a way that could imply Neurodesk endorses or certifies your product.

Neurodesk is a community project and generally says yes — asking is mostly so we know it is happening.

Full detail is in BRAND.md.

Naming and capitalisation guidelines

Section titled “Naming and capitalisation guidelines”

To ensure consistency in our branding, please follow the guidelines below when referring to Neurodesk and its associated tools.

Correct usage

Section titled “Correct usage”
  • Neurodesk: capitalise only the first letter.
    • neurodesk or NEURODESK may be used where the surrounding style calls for it, such as an all-caps heading or a lowercase URL.
  • Use “Neurodesk” to refer to the overall project, platform, or community.
  • Use consistent casing in product names:
    • Neurodesktop
    • Neurocommand
    • Neurocontainers
    • NeurodeskApp

Do not use

Section titled “Do not use”

Avoid the following incorrect variations:

  • Variants with an internal capital letter
  • Neuro Desk — space in the middle
  • neurodesk in running prose, titles, or headings, where Neurodesk is correct

Typography

Section titled “Typography”

Scirich Regular

Section titled “Scirich Regular”

Scirich is a modified version of Aldrich by Matt McInerney, adapted and renamed by Michèle Masson-Trottier. It is licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, as is the original.

  • Download Scirich-Regular.ttf — installable font
  • Download Scirich-Regular.sfd — FontForge source, for editing
  • Upstream: micmas/Scirich-font

If you redistribute it, the OFL requires you to include the full licence, avoid the reserved name “Aldrich”, attribute the original designer, and describe your changes.

When exporting a logo or figure to SVG, convert text to paths so it renders correctly for people who do not have Scirich installed.

Contributing an asset

Section titled “Contributing an asset”

To add a figure, diagram, or screenshot to the shared library, see CONTRIBUTING.md in the media repository. In short: use the naming convention, commit a vector master where one exists, keep files under the size limits, and update the folder’s README table.
